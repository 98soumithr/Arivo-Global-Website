## Product image prompts

Each product needs three photographs. The **diagram** slot is already handled by inline SVGs.

## Style direction (apply to all prompts)

- **Studio shots** (hero + detail): seamless off-white (#F4F4F3) ground, single soft key light from upper left, gentle fill from the right, no hard shadows. Clean commercial product photography. The product should be the only thing in frame — no hands, no props, no text overlays. Shoot at roughly 3:2 landscape for hero, 4:3 for detail.
- **Context shots**: industrial environment, natural available light plus overhead factory lighting, warm tones from molten metal or furnace glow where applicable. Photojournalistic style, not stock-photo feel. 16:9 landscape.
- **Resolution**: generate at the highest available resolution; we will crop to 1600 × 1067 (hero), 1200 × 900 (detail), and 1600 × 900 (context).

## Tips for Google ImageFX / Flow

1. **Copy each prompt exactly** — don't shorten it. The detail matters for getting the right industrial product.
2. If the first generation doesn't look right, try adding "professional commercial product photography, 50mm lens, f/8" to the studio shots.
3. For context shots, adding "editorial industrial photography, Canon EOS R5, available light" can help.
4. Generate 4 variants of each and pick the most realistic one.
5. After generating, crop to the target aspect ratio before placing in `public/images/products/<slug>/`.
6. The hero images are the most important — they appear on the product cards and the product page header.
