# Lessons

## 2026-09-27 — Published company details
- **Pattern:** Specs can require publishing identifying details (registration numbers, address, phone, counts) that the owner does not want public.
- **Rule:** Before rendering statutory or contact identifiers (CIN, GSTIN, IEC, registered address, phone/WhatsApp, customer or country counts), confirm with the owner that they are to be published, even when the spec lists them. For Arivo Global: none of these appear on the site; contact is the RFQ form and the enquiries mailbox.

## 2026-09-27 — Design-system details the owner rejected
- **Pattern:** Decorative conventions from the design system (the 28×2 rule before every section label) read as "template / AI-generated" to the owner, and region lists read as a restriction on where Arivo sells.
- **Rule:** Keep section labels plain text. Describe markets as open-ended ("worldwide, including Europe, the USA, the Gulf and Southeast Asia"), never as a closed list. Scroll-reveal motion is wanted — keep it restrained (once per block, small offset, reduced-motion respected).
